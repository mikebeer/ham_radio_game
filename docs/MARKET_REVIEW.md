# Similar apps (checked 2026-09-30)

Both apps below are by the same developer (Yousif AL Balushi / Unixeer), free, no account, no ads, no tracking,
iOS 15+ only as far as the App Store and web search show. No Google Play listing was found.
(The Play Store page could not be fetched in this session, so Android availability is unconfirmed.)

## Ham Learning (App Store id 6782169770, v1.0.2)
* FCC question pools, Technician / General / Extra (1,400+ questions), mock exams, per-subelement mastery.
* Hundreds of lessons, Q-codes, phonetic alphabet, glossary, band charts.
* Koch Morse trainer with adjustable spacing and speed, on-device CW decoder, tap-key paddle.
* WebSDR list, shortwave listening logbook with CSV export.
* Calculators: wavelength, dipole length, Ohm's law, dB, SWR, LC, coax loss, RF exposure, Maidenhead grid; circuit and antenna labs.
* UI in 9 languages, but the content is US-only. Offline-first, text size up to 200 %.

## Ham Gaming (App Store id 6782177779, v1.1.1)
* Bite-sized mini-games: phonetic alphabet, Q-codes, Morse, callsign prefixes, bands, modes, speed drills.
* Full rig simulator: 14 bands, VFO knob, waterfall, S-meter, filters, PTT with station replies.
* Modes: Missions, Fox Hunt, QRM Drill, Contest, free play. Voice games with speech recognition.
* Satellite tracking with phone motion, antenna/propagation labs, Yagi design, NanoVNA simulation.
* 10 languages incl. Arabic (RTL).

## Where CQ Quest differs
* Story progression for beginners: four goals, a shack you build part by part, a guided first QSO, callsign directory.
* Legal content per country (DE official pool, AT, CH, US, UK primers), not US only.
* Open source, Godot (desktop, web, mobile), not iOS only.

## Ideas worth borrowing
* Callsign-prefix game and Fox Hunt mini-game; Q-code and phonetic drills.
* Calculators (Ohm, wavelength, dipole) inside the shack lessons.
* CSV/ADIF export of the logbook; text-size setting; more UI languages; explicit "no tracking" statement.
